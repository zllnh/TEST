About Us

Learn about who we are and why we do what we do.

We started as a small startup making a few shirts we actually like. Some things and agreements fell out of place, and now we’ve been working the brand from the ground up.

Our name, VANDLZ, reflects the rugged, youthful energy that stems from the streets of Luzon, and that energy is something we aim to carry into our clothing.
